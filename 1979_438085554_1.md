## 3. Challenges and Solutions  
Challenges

Two of the group members had no prior experience with programming, which made much of the work challenging. We were unsure what the final product was supposed to look like, especially since the lecturer was coding a different app than the one we were developing ourselves. This made it difficult to follow the lectures while also programming independently.

Time constraints were another major challenge, as all group members have families and full-time jobs alongside their studies. The work was also highly dependent on computer access, and we lost valuable time due to technical issues—especially when prompts and development tools did not function as expected.

Solutions

We addressed these challenges by contacting both the course coordinator and the support teacher for guidance. We also made active use of communication within the group through Microsoft Teams. Early in the semester, we created our own Teams channel where we held regular meetings and maintained a low threshold for asking questions and helping each other. This continuous collaboration helped us move forward despite the difficulties.
### 3.1 Technical Challenges  

#### **Challenge 1: Structuring the project**
The team was unsure about how to correctly structure both the frontend and backend folders.

**Solution:**  
Gemini suggested a component-based frontend structure with clear separation of concerns, and an MVC-inspired folder structure for backend.

**AI role:**  
Crucial — provided example structures and diagrams.

---

#### **Challenge 2: Technology selection**
Choosing frameworks was difficult due to limited experience.

**Solution:**  
Gemini compared multiple frameworks (React vs. Svelte vs. Vue, and Flask vs. Node.js vs. Express). Based on simplicity and flexibility, React + Node.js + Supabase was chosen.

**AI role:**  
Provided a meaningful evaluation and argumentation for each choice.

---

### 3.2 Collaboration Challenges
- One group member left early  
- Coordination became harder  
- Task planning required restructuring  

**Solutions:**  
- Reassigning responsibilities  
- More frequent team meetings

### 3.3 AI-Specific Challenges
- Some code suggestions from Gemini were incorrect

**Solutions:**

- Team discussions before accepting AI suggestions  
- Simplifying AI suggestions to fit the project scope

## 4. Critical Evaluation of AI Assistance  

### 4.1 Benefits of AI Assistance  

**Efficiency:**  
- Faster planning  
- Faster documentation  
- Suggested ready-to-use code structures  

**Learning:**  
- AI explained code concepts  
- Helped break down large problems  
- Enabled learning about backend architecture 
- learned how to challenge the AI suggestions before putting them into the final project 

**Code quality:**  
- Cleaner naming conventions  
- Consistent structure  
- Better initial architecture  

---

### 4.2 Limitations of AI  

**Quality & reliability issues:**  
- Some code did not work  
- Some suggestions were outdated  

**Overreliance risk:**  
- Certain tasks risked being dominated by AI suggestions  
- Team had to consciously maintain independent thinking  

**Reduced creativity:**  
- AI sometimes pushed the group toward certain solutions  
- Important to keep room for human creativity  

---

### 4.3 With vs. Without AI  

**With AI:**  
- Faster work  
- More structured code  
- More supervission needed  

**Without AI:**  
- Longer planning time  
- Greater uncertainty  
- More trial-and-error  

---

### 4.4 Overall Assessment  
AI has been **overwhelmingly beneficial**, but only when used critically and with human supervission. The AI has been a tool, not the selution 
The team learned:

> “AI is a tool — not a replacement for human understanding.”
